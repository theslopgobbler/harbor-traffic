ROUTETIMES SEENDAYSBUSESMAP

On time = leaving a timed stop 0 to 5 minutes after its scheduled time, measured from the bus's GPS track passing the stop (early = before the scheduled time; late = more than 5 minutes after). Each one notes the weather at the nearest weather station and any WSDOT road alert within 1.5 km of the stop at that moment. Off route = a moving bus more than about a block from its route's line. Off-route spots seen on several days are shortcuts or standing detours; one-day spots are usually a one-off detour or the tracker listing a bus on the wrong route. Every position is kept, for rebuilding the routes from where buses actually drive.
