WHERE BUSES LEAVE THEIR ROUTES

ROUTETIMES SEENDAYSBUSESMAP

On time = leaving a timed stop 0 to 5 minutes after its scheduled time, measured from the bus's GPS track passing the stop (early = before the scheduled time; late = more than 5 minutes after). Only buses GHT's tracker lists as running a route count; parked and out-of-service buses are left out. Which scheduled time a bus was leaving for: "trip" = the time the tracker said that bus was headed for (it knows the bus's trip); "nearest" = the stop's closest scheduled time, used when the tracker didn't say. No bus seen = 95 minutes after a scheduled time (a bus up to 90 minutes late still counts as late), no in-service bus had passed within 80 m of that stop, on a route that had a bus out that day (it can also mean the bus's GPS was off, so treat one-offs with care; a stop that shows up every day probably has its location wrong in the tracker). Times come from the tracker's timetable for that day. Each one notes the weather at the nearest weather station and any WSDOT road alert within 1.5 km of the stop at that moment. Off route = a moving bus more than about a block from its route's line. Off-route spots seen on several days are shortcuts or standing detours; one-day spots are usually a one-off detour or the tracker listing a bus on the wrong route. Every position is kept, for rebuilding the routes from where buses actually drive.

DAYVISITORSPAGE VIEWS

Updates every 30 seconds. "Watching now" is open pages that checked in during the last 3 minutes (pages in a background tab don't count). A visit is a page opened in a new tab; the page's own reloads after an update don't count again. Only random tab IDs are kept, nothing about who.

Mark each of your own devices (phone, computer) once, so your own visits don't count. Visits already counted stay.

MAP CRASHES ON THIS DEVICE

The map's last minute before each time it closed without saying goodbye (a crash, or a phone killing the tab). Kept in this browser only.
