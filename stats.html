On time = passing a timed stop 0 to 5 minutes after its scheduled time (the tracker's "next stop" switching to the stop after). Off route = a moving bus more than about a block from its route's line, checked once a minute. Off-route spots seen on several days are shortcuts or standing detours; one-day spots are usually a one-off detour or the tracker listing a bus on the wrong route.
