WHAT WAS IT?
From Grays Harbor Transit's GPS (the relay logs it 3 times a minute). Usual speeds are the middle speed buses drove each ~300 m of road, by direction, from slow-baseline.json. A flat line in the space-time chart is a bus standing still; a steep one is a bus moving fast. A slowdown that builds and clears shows up as a growing, then shrinking, patch of orange and red.
