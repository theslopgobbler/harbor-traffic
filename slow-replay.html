WHAT WAS IT?
PATTERNS: every logged slowdown, grouped by spot (within 500 m). The bars show when they happened: weekdays on top, weekends below, darker for more. The chart lays every slowdown at the spot on the same road, each in its own color, thick where it was slow: lined up by when each one started, or by the time of day. Tap a date to replay that one. SKIPPED BEFORE: a spot the map used to leave out as routine (until Nov 14).
From Grays Harbor Transit's GPS (the relay logs it 3 times a minute). Usual speeds are the middle speed buses drove each ~300 m of road, by direction, from slow-baseline.json. A flat line in the space-time chart is a bus standing still; a steep one is a bus moving fast. A slowdown that builds and clears shows up as a growing, then shrinking, patch of orange and red.
